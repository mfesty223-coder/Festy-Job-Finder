About the Opportunity

An online tutoring opportunity is available for individuals who enjoy teaching and helping students learn.

Tutors can teach students online from home and choose a schedule that works with their availability.

Key Responsibilities

  • Teach students through online lessons.
  • Prepare and deliver engaging lessons.
  • Explain concepts clearly to students.
  • Help students improve their knowledge and skills.
  • Communicate professionally with students.
  • Maintain a reliable teaching schedule.
  • Provide a positive and supportive learning experience.

Requirements

  • Strong communication skills.
  • Good command of English.
  • Ability to explain information clearly.
  • Reliable internet connection.
  • Computer, laptop or suitable device for online lessons.
  • Quiet environment suitable for teaching.
  • Professional and friendly attitude.

Skills That Can Help You Succeed

  • Teaching
  • Communication
  • Patience
  • Active listening
  • Time management
  • Online communication

Flexible Online Work

This opportunity may suit people looking for flexible online work, including individuals who want to teach from home or work around other commitments.

Before You Apply

Make sure your CV or tutor profile is updated and that you have the equipment and internet connection needed to teach online.

Applications are completed through the external tutoring platform after clicking the application button below.

Ready to Apply?

If you meet the requirements, continue to the external application platform.
