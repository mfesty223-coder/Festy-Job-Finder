About the Role

An exciting Sales Consultant opportunity is available for an enthusiastic and customer-focused individual.

The successful applicant will assist customers, identify their needs and provide suitable products and services while building strong professional relationships.

Key Responsibilities

  • Engage with new and existing customers.
  • Identify customer needs and provide appropriate solutions.
  • Promote products and services.
  • Build and maintain strong customer relationships.
  • Follow up with potential customers.
  • Meet sales and performance targets.
  • Maintain professional customer service standards.

Requirements

  • Good communication skills.
  • Customer-focused approach.
  • Strong interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work towards targets.
  • Good problem-solving abilities.
  • Professional and positive attitude.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Skills That Can Help You Succeed

  • Problem solving
