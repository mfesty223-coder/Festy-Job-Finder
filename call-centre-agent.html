About the Role

We are looking for motivated and customer-focused individuals for a Call Centre Agent opportunity.

The successful applicant will communicate with customers, respond to enquiries and provide professional assistance while maintaining a high standard of customer service.

Key Responsibilities

  • Answer incoming customer calls.
  • Assist customers with enquiries and requests.
  • Provide accurate information to customers.
  • Handle customer concerns in a professional manner.
  • Record and update customer information when required.
  • Follow workplace procedures and customer service standards.
  • Work towards service and performance targets.

Requirements

  • Good communication skills.
  • Professional telephone manner.
  • Customer-focused attitude.
  • Ability to work under pressure.
  • Basic computer literacy.
  • Good problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a team.

Skills That Can Help You Succeed

  • Communication
  • Active listening
  • Customer service
  • Problem solving
  • Computer skills
  • Time management
