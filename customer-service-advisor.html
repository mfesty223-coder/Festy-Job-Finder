About the Role

An exciting Customer Service Advisor opportunity is available for individuals who enjoy helping customers and providing professional customer service.

The successful applicants will assist customers, respond to enquiries and provide support while maintaining a high standard of service.

Key Responsibilities

  • Handle customer enquiries and requests.
  • Provide professional and helpful customer service.
  • Respond to customer queries in a timely manner.
  • Assist customers with their service-related needs.
  • Resolve customer concerns where possible.
  • Maintain accurate customer information and records.
  • Work with internal teams to assist customers.

Requirements

  • Good communication skills.
  • Strong customer service skills.
  • Professional and positive attitude.
  • Good problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to communicate effectively with customers.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment.

Skills That Can Help You Succeed

  • Customer service
  • Communication
  • Problem solving
  • Active listening
  • Time management
  • Attention to detail
