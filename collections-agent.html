About the Role

A Collections Agent is responsible for communicating with customers regarding outstanding accounts and assisting them with suitable payment arrangements.

The role requires excellent communication, professionalism and the ability to handle customer conversations confidently.

Key Responsibilities

  • Contact customers regarding outstanding accounts.
  • Discuss account information and payment options.
  • Assist customers with suitable payment arrangements.
  • Maintain professional and respectful customer interactions.
  • Record customer interactions accurately.
  • Follow up on outstanding accounts.
  • Work towards collection and performance targets.

Requirements

  • Good verbal and written communication skills.
  • Strong customer service skills.
  • Professional telephone manner.
  • Ability to work under pressure.
  • Good listening and negotiation skills.
  • Strong attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Skills That Can Help You Succeed

  • Customer service
  • Communication
  • Negotiation
  • Problem solving
  • Active listening
  • Time management
