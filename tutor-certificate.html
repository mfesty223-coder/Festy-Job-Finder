120-Hour TEFL Certificate

Looking to improve your teaching skills or start teaching English online? You can complete a free online 120-hour TEFL course and work towards earning a certificate.

COURSE LENGTH 120 Hours
COST 100% Free
STUDY FORMAT Online
Study from anywhere: The course is available online, allowing you to study at a time and place that works for you.

What You'll Learn

  • Lesson planning
  • English grammar
  • Teaching listening and reading
  • Teaching speaking and writing
  • Classroom teaching techniques
  • Teaching children
  • Practical classroom survival tips

Course Requirements

  • Ability to communicate clearly and fluently in English
  • Native nationality is not required
  • A university degree is not required
  • Access to the internet and a device for completing the online course

Online Course Access

The course can be completed online and studied at your own pace. Online campus access is provided so you can work through the course materials.

After completing the required course work, you can proceed with the certificate process.
